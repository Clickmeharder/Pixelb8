ExploreWASD moves. R toggles Run / Walk. Hold Shift + wheel for variable crouch, L leans, X sits, Space jumps, I opens inventory.
CabinetsWalk close and press Enter. The real game canvas is rendered live on the cabinet. Backspace steps away, so Escape remains available to the game.
VR / WebXRUse the headset VR button. Left thumbstick moves. Point/trigger can interact with doors and cabinets.
PoolWalk around normally. Hold left mouse only when taking a stroke. L leans forward; pool adds Cue and Bridge to the hotbar for long-reach shots.
